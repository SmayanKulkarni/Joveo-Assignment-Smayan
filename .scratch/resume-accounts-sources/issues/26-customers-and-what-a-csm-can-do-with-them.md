## Question

What is a Customer record, and what can its CSM do with it?

- What it holds: a name typed by the CSM, and anything else that earns its place, such as a note or contact details. Say why for each field, since each one is a stranger's personal data.
- Creating a Customer: by hand first, or from a resume? Can two Customers share a name, and how does a CSM tell them apart?
- Listing, renaming and deleting. Deleting is required and takes everything stored about the Customer with it: say how it reaches resumes, profile, Store, Runs, checkpoints and the replay cache. Only test resumes are ever held, so the record needs no consent fields.
- A CSM who leaves: reassigning is out of scope, so what happens to their Customers?
- Another CSM's Customer is refused the same way another CSM's Run is. Endpoints and refusals, in the style of the Web UI LLD's API section.
- Where the table lives, beside the CSM tables.
- The LLDs must use Customer the way `GLOSSARY.md` does, and Client is retired by [What a Resume is for](17-what-a-resume-is-for.md).
